Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to build a G+1 house?

A G+1 home on a 30×40 plot usually takes 9 to 11 months from design sign-off, including about 6 weeks for plan approval.

Is the quoted price really fixed?

Yes. The per sq.ft rate in your contract stays the same for the whole project. It changes only if you ask for changes to the design or specification.

How do payments work?

You pay a booking amount, then in stages linked to finished work: foundation, each slab, masonry, finishes and handover. Each payment is released after inspection.

Do you handle BBMP / BDA approvals?

Yes. We prepare the drawings and apply for plan sanction and temporary electricity and water connections in every package.

Can I choose my own tiles, fittings or paint?

Yes. Each package includes an allowance for these items. If you pick something above the allowance, you pay only the difference.
